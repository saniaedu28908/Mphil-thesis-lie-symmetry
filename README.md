## Abstract

This research considers two nonlinear partial differential equations, the modified Soliton–Burgers equation and the modified Allen–Cahn equation. The Soliton–Burgers equation is analyzed using both classical and non-classical symmetry methods to obtain invariant solutions, while the Allen–Cahn equation is treated using the classical Lie symmetry approach. The invariant solutions obtained from both models are presented graphically to illustrate their structural behaviour and spatial-temporal evolution. These graphical representations provide a clear visualization of wave propagation, phase transition, and exponential-type dynamics under different parameter settings. Furthermore, the invariant solutions of the Soliton–Burgers equation are utilized in image scrambling and encryption, establishing a connection between symmetry analysis and computational imaging. In addition, the dynamical behaviour of the solutions is examined to study the effect of parameter variations on solution structures, revealing different qualitative regimes including stable wave patterns, phase transition behaviour, and transient exponential dynamics. The results demonstrate how symmetry-based methods not only simplify nonlinear partial differential equations but also provide meaningful visual and computational interpretations in applied mathematics.

**Keywords:** Lie group methods, symmetry analysis, nonlinear partial differential equations, classical symmetries, non-classical symmetries, invariant solutions, image scrambling, visual encryption.
